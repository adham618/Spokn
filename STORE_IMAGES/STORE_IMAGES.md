## Tips

- **Recraft** and **Ideogram** both support image reference input — best choices here
- **Midjourney**: paste your screenshot in Discord then use `--cref [image url]`
- For screenshots (4–8): the easiest approach is just taking real Chrome screenshots with the extension loaded and cropping to 1280×800 — no AI needed
- Use **Figma** (search "Chrome browser mockup" in Community) to composite real screenshots into a polished browser frame
